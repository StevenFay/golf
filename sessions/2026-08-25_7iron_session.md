## Coaching read

**Diagnosis:** Grip-change transition dip, not a failed grip. After the switch, faces opened to 7–10°, closure rate dropped (~1,815–1,863°/s vs 2,400+ on the good swings), dynamic loft climbed to 32–36°, and club speed fell ~5 km/h — the signature of steering/holding the face off rather than releasing it. Speed was being converted into height and spin (up to 7,270 rpm back spin) instead of distance, which is what "weak, no power" felt like.

**Counter-evidence that the grip works:** shot 2 (old grip) face 0.4° closed, baby draw, smash 1.35, 139.9 m; shots 11 and 14 (new grip) face 0.9° / 1.7°, near-dead-straight, smash 1.32 / 1.24. When released, the stronger grip delivered a square face.

**Secondary observations:**
- Pace: 16 shots in ~6 minutes. No reset between swings; late-session raggedness (shot 16 at 114 km/h, shot 15 thin at −16.2 mm) is consistent with rapid-fire fatigue.
- Dynamic lie averaged ~7° toe-down (range 4–9.7°) — an independent right-bias contributor. Equipment/setup item, flag for fitting review when the new irons arrive.
- Contact scatter is wide: impact height −16.2 to +20.6 mm, width −21.5 to +30.6 mm.

**Prescription for next session:**
1. First 15–20 min: 9-to-3 half swings with the 2.5-knuckle grip at 60–70% effort, feeling the toe rotate through.
2. Targets: face angle 0–3° open, side spin < 500 rpm. Ignore carry distance entirely.
3. One ball per 45–60 s, regrip fresh each time. Progress to full swings only after three consecutive half-swings hit the targets.
4. Expect 2+ weeks before power fully returns.
